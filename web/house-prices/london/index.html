The average house price in the London region of England is £554,462 as of August 2026. This is what they have cost since January 1995, in today's money rather than in the cash prices of the day.

Over the last 10 years the average home in London has lost 22.9% of its value in real terms, even though the cash price is 9.7% higher. Adjusted for inflation, home prices in London peaked in July 2017 and are 23.8% below that today.

The average detached price in the London region of England is £1,164,455 as of August 2026. This is what they have cost since January 1995, in today's money rather than in the cash prices of the day.
