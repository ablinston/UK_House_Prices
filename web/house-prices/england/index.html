The average house price in England is £293,550 as of August 2026. This is what they have cost since January 1995, in today's money rather than in the cash prices of the day.

Over the last 10 years the average home in England has lost 5.5% of its value in real terms, even though the cash price is 34.5% higher. Adjusted for inflation, home prices in England peaked in June 2021 and are 14.5% below that today.

The average detached price in England is £472,677 as of August 2026. This is what they have cost since January 1995, in today's money rather than in the cash prices of the day.

Over the last 10 years the average detached home in England has lost 4.6% of its value in real terms, even though the cash price is 35.7% higher. Adjusted for inflation, detached home prices in England peaked in January 2022 and are 14.1% below that today.

The average semi-detached price in England is £291,974 as of August 2026. This is what they have cost since January 1995, in today's money rather than in the cash prices of the day.

Over the last 10 years the average semi-detached home in England has gained 0.5% in real terms, on a cash price 43.0% higher. Adjusted for inflation, semi-detached home prices in England peaked in September 2021 and are 11.1% below that today.

The average terraced price in England is £247,369 as of August 2026. This is what they have cost since January 1995, in today's money rather than in the cash prices of the day.

Over the last 10 years the average terraced home in England has lost 1.2% of its value in real terms, even though the cash price is 40.7% higher. Adjusted for inflation, terraced home prices in England peaked in June 2021 and are 13.3% below that today.

The average flat price in England is £218,560 as of August 2026. This is what they have cost since January 1995, in today's money rather than in the cash prices of the day.

Over the last 10 years the average flat home in England has lost 21.4% of its value in real terms, even though the cash price is 11.9% higher. Adjusted for inflation, flat home prices in England peaked in June 2021 and are 24.0% below that today.
