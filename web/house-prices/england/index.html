England house prices, adjusted for inflation

Average price, June 2026 £293,249
Real change, 10 years -4.0%
Below its June 2021 peak -13.9%

The average house price in England is £293,249 as of June 2026. This is what homes have cost there since January 1995, in today's money rather than in the cash prices of the day.

Over the last 10 years the average home in England has lost 4.0% of its value in real terms, even though the cash price is 36.0% higher. Adjusted for inflation, prices in England peaked in June 2021 and are 13.9% below that today.
