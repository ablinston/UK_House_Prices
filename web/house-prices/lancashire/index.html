The average house price in Lancashire, England, is £193,813 as of September 2026. This is what they have cost since January 1995, in today's money rather than in the cash prices of the day.

Over the last 10 years the average home in Lancashire has gained 3.2% in real terms, on a cash price 47.2% higher. Adjusted for inflation, home prices in Lancashire peaked in September 2007 and are 21.8% below that today.

The average flat price in Lancashire, England, is £113,338 as of September 2026. This is what they have cost since January 1995, in today's money rather than in the cash prices of the day.
