The average house price in Lancashire, England, is £193,687 as of July 2026. This is what they have cost since January 1995, in today's money rather than in the cash prices of the day.

Over the last 10 years the average home in Lancashire has gained 4.3% in real terms, on a cash price 48.1% higher. Adjusted for inflation, home prices in Lancashire peaked in September 2007 and are 21.2% below that today.

The average terraced price in Lancashire, England, is £150,172 as of July 2026. This is what they have cost since January 1995, in today's money rather than in the cash prices of the day.

The average flat price in Lancashire, England, is £114,348 as of July 2026. This is what they have cost since January 1995, in today's money rather than in the cash prices of the day.
