The average house price in the West Midlands Region of England is £250,938 as of June 2026. This is what they have cost since January 1995, in today's money rather than in the cash prices of the day.

Over the last 10 years the average home in West Midlands Region has gained 4.7% in real terms, on a cash price 48.3% higher. Adjusted for inflation, home prices in West Midlands Region peaked in June 2021 and are 11.2% below that today.

The average detached price in the West Midlands Region of England is £423,577 as of June 2026. This is what they have cost since January 1995, in today's money rather than in the cash prices of the day.

Over the last 10 years the average detached home in West Midlands Region has gained 3.4% in real terms, on a cash price 46.5% higher. Adjusted for inflation, detached home prices in West Midlands Region peaked in June 2021 and are 9.9% below that today.

The average semi-detached price in the West Midlands Region of England is £254,511 as of June 2026. This is what they have cost since January 1995, in today's money rather than in the cash prices of the day.

The average terraced price in the West Midlands Region of England is £204,880 as of June 2026. This is what they have cost since January 1995, in today's money rather than in the cash prices of the day.

The average flat price in the West Midlands Region of England is £139,145 as of June 2026. This is what they have cost since January 1995, in today's money rather than in the cash prices of the day.

Over the last 10 years the average flat home in West Midlands Region has lost 8.3% of its value in real terms, even though the cash price is 29.8% higher. Adjusted for inflation, flat home prices in West Midlands Region peaked in December 2007 and are 26.3% below that today.
