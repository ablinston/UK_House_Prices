The average house price in the West Midlands Region of England is £250,892 as of July 2026. This is what they have cost since January 1995, in today's money rather than in the cash prices of the day.

Over the last 10 years the average home in West Midlands Region has gained 4.2% in real terms, on a cash price 48.1% higher. Adjusted for inflation, home prices in West Midlands Region peaked in June 2021 and are 11.5% below that today.

The average terraced price in the West Midlands Region of England is £205,553 as of July 2026. This is what they have cost since January 1995, in today's money rather than in the cash prices of the day.

The average flat price in the West Midlands Region of England is £138,823 as of July 2026. This is what they have cost since January 1995, in today's money rather than in the cash prices of the day.
