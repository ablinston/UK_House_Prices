The average house price in the West Midlands Region of England is £250,752 as of August 2026. This is what they have cost since January 1995, in today's money rather than in the cash prices of the day.

Over the last 10 years the average home in West Midlands Region has gained 2.8% in real terms, on a cash price 46.3% higher. Adjusted for inflation, home prices in West Midlands Region peaked in June 2021 and are 12.0% below that today.

The average flat price in the West Midlands Region of England is £138,502 as of August 2026. This is what they have cost since January 1995, in today's money rather than in the cash prices of the day.
