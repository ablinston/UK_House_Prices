The average house price in the West Midlands Region of England is £250,334 as of September 2026. This is what they have cost since January 1995, in today's money rather than in the cash prices of the day.

Over the last 10 years the average home in West Midlands Region has gained 2.6% in real terms, on a cash price 46.2% higher. Adjusted for inflation, home prices in West Midlands Region peaked in June 2021 and are 12.4% below that today.

The average detached price in the West Midlands Region of England is £422,837 as of September 2026. This is what they have cost since January 1995, in today's money rather than in the cash prices of the day.

Over the last 10 years the average detached home in West Midlands Region has gained 0.9% in real terms, on a cash price 43.8% higher. Adjusted for inflation, detached home prices in West Midlands Region peaked in June 2021 and are 11.0% below that today.

The average semi-detached price in the West Midlands Region of England is £254,329 as of September 2026. This is what they have cost since January 1995, in today's money rather than in the cash prices of the day.

Over the last 10 years the average semi-detached home in West Midlands Region has gained 6.1% in real terms, on a cash price 51.2% higher. Adjusted for inflation, semi-detached home prices in West Midlands Region peaked in June 2021 and are 9.9% below that today.

The average terraced price in the West Midlands Region of England is £204,880 as of September 2026. This is what they have cost since January 1995, in today's money rather than in the cash prices of the day.

Over the last 10 years the average terraced home in West Midlands Region has gained 5.6% in real terms, on a cash price 50.5% higher. Adjusted for inflation, terraced home prices in West Midlands Region peaked in June 2021 and are 12.8% below that today.

The average flat price in the West Midlands Region of England is £137,024 as of September 2026. This is what they have cost since January 1995, in today's money rather than in the cash prices of the day.

Over the last 10 years the average flat home in West Midlands Region has lost 11.3% of its value in real terms, even though the cash price is 26.5% higher. Adjusted for inflation, flat home prices in West Midlands Region peaked in December 2007 and are 28.2% below that today.
