The average house price in Nottinghamshire, England, is £230,374 as of September 2026. This is what they have cost since January 1995, in today's money rather than in the cash prices of the day.

Over the last 10 years the average home in Nottinghamshire has gained 4.5% in real terms, on a cash price 48.9% higher. Adjusted for inflation, home prices in Nottinghamshire peaked in September 2022 and are 12.4% below that today.

The average detached price in Nottinghamshire, England, is £342,722 as of September 2026. This is what they have cost since January 1995, in today's money rather than in the cash prices of the day.

The average semi-detached price in Nottinghamshire, England, is £212,358 as of September 2026. This is what they have cost since January 1995, in today's money rather than in the cash prices of the day.

The average terraced price in Nottinghamshire, England, is £166,227 as of September 2026. This is what they have cost since January 1995, in today's money rather than in the cash prices of the day.

Over the last 10 years the average terraced home in Nottinghamshire has gained 6.2% in real terms, on a cash price 51.3% higher. Adjusted for inflation, terraced home prices in Nottinghamshire peaked in September 2022 and are 12.2% below that today.

The average flat price in Nottinghamshire, England, is £116,504 as of September 2026. This is what they have cost since January 1995, in today's money rather than in the cash prices of the day.

Over the last 10 years the average flat home in Nottinghamshire has lost 7.9% of its value in real terms, even though the cash price is 31.2% higher. Adjusted for inflation, flat home prices in Nottinghamshire peaked in November 2007 and are 29.6% below that today.
