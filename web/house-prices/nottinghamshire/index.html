Nottinghamshire house prices, adjusted for inflation

The average house price in Nottinghamshire, England, is £232,380 as of June 2026. This is what homes have cost there since January 1995, in today's money rather than in the cash prices of the day.

Average price, June 2026 £232,380
Real change since the peak -7.3%

The average home in Nottinghamshire costs 7.3% less in real terms than at the September 2007 peak, even though the cash price is 61.3% higher.
