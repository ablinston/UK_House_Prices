The average house price in Kent, England, is £345,186 as of August 2026. This is what they have cost since January 1995, in today's money rather than in the cash prices of the day.

Over the last 10 years the average home in Kent has lost 10.9% of its value in real terms, even though the cash price is 26.8% higher. Adjusted for inflation, home prices in Kent peaked in February 2022 and are 17.4% below that today.
