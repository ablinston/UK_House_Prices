The average house price in Kent, England, is £346,933 as of July 2026. This is what they have cost since January 1995, in today's money rather than in the cash prices of the day.

Over the last 10 years the average home in Kent has lost 9.2% of its value in real terms, even though the cash price is 29.0% higher. Adjusted for inflation, home prices in Kent peaked in February 2022 and are 16.6% below that today.
