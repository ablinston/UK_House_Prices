The average house price in Merseyside, England, is £200,992 as of June 2026. This is what homes have cost there since January 1995, in today's money rather than in the cash prices of the day.

Over the last 10 years the average home in Merseyside has gained 14.3% in real terms, on a cash price 61.9% higher. Adjusted for inflation, prices in Merseyside peaked in July 2007 and are 13.3% below that today.
