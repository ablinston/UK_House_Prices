The average house price in Leicestershire, England, is £283,211 as of July 2026. This is what they have cost since January 1995, in today's money rather than in the cash prices of the day.

Over the last 10 years the average home in Leicestershire has gained 4.1% in real terms, on a cash price 47.8% higher. Adjusted for inflation, home prices in Leicestershire peaked in September 2022 and are 11.6% below that today.

The average flat price in Leicestershire, England, is £131,700 as of July 2026. This is what they have cost since January 1995, in today's money rather than in the cash prices of the day.
