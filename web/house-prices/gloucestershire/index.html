The average house price in Gloucestershire, England, is £318,044 as of June 2026. This is what homes have cost there since January 1995, in today's money rather than in the cash prices of the day.

Over the last 10 years the average home in Gloucestershire has gained 1.1% in real terms, on a cash price 43.3% higher. Adjusted for inflation, home prices in Gloucestershire peaked in September 2022 and are 12.8% below that today.

The average detached price in Gloucestershire, England, is £520,110 as of June 2026. This is what homes have cost there since January 1995, in today's money rather than in the cash prices of the day.

Over the last 10 years the average detached home in Gloucestershire has gained 2.9% in real terms, on a cash price 45.7% higher. Adjusted for inflation, detached home prices in Gloucestershire peaked in September 2022 and are 12.7% below that today.

The average semi-detached price in Gloucestershire, England, is £327,686 as of June 2026. This is what homes have cost there since January 1995, in today's money rather than in the cash prices of the day.

Over the last 10 years the average semi-detached home in Gloucestershire has gained 4.7% in real terms, on a cash price 48.4% higher. Adjusted for inflation, semi-detached home prices in Gloucestershire peaked in September 2022 and are 11.2% below that today.

The average terraced price in Gloucestershire, England, is £267,135 as of June 2026. This is what homes have cost there since January 1995, in today's money rather than in the cash prices of the day.

Over the last 10 years the average terraced home in Gloucestershire has gained 1.7% in real terms, on a cash price 44.0% higher. Adjusted for inflation, terraced home prices in Gloucestershire peaked in September 2022 and are 12.6% below that today.

The average flat price in Gloucestershire, England, is £172,340 as of June 2026. This is what homes have cost there since January 1995, in today's money rather than in the cash prices of the day.

Over the last 10 years the average flat home in Gloucestershire has lost 12.0% of its value in real terms, even though the cash price is 24.7% higher. Adjusted for inflation, flat home prices in Gloucestershire peaked in September 2007 and are 26.4% below that today.
