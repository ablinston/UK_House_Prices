The average house price in Scotland is £197,968 as of September 2026. This is what they have cost since January 1995, in today's money rather than in the cash prices of the day.

Over the last 10 years the average home in Scotland has gained 2.7% in real terms, on a cash price 46.4% higher. Adjusted for inflation, home prices in Scotland peaked in August 2007 and are 18.5% below that today.

The average detached price in Scotland is £368,847 as of September 2026. This is what they have cost since January 2004, in today's money rather than in the cash prices of the day.

Over the last 10 years the average detached home in Scotland has gained 5.2% in real terms, on a cash price 50.0% higher. Adjusted for inflation, detached home prices in Scotland peaked in August 2007 and are 13.2% below that today.

The average semi-detached price in Scotland is £226,204 as of September 2026. This is what they have cost since January 2004, in today's money rather than in the cash prices of the day.

Over the last 10 years the average semi-detached home in Scotland has gained 6.1% in real terms, on a cash price 51.3% higher. Adjusted for inflation, semi-detached home prices in Scotland peaked in August 2007 and are 12.5% below that today.

The average terraced price in Scotland is £183,854 as of September 2026. This is what they have cost since January 2004, in today's money rather than in the cash prices of the day.

Over the last 10 years the average terraced home in Scotland has gained 7.2% in real terms, on a cash price 52.8% higher. Adjusted for inflation, terraced home prices in Scotland peaked in May 2008 and are 13.4% below that today.

The average flat price in Scotland is £137,777 as of September 2026. This is what they have cost since January 2004, in today's money rather than in the cash prices of the day.

Over the last 10 years the average flat home in Scotland has lost 2.9% of its value in real terms, even though the cash price is 38.4% higher. Adjusted for inflation, flat home prices in Scotland peaked in June 2007 and are 28.0% below that today.
