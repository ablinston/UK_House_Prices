Greater Manchester house prices, adjusted for inflation

Average price, June 2026 £240,130
Real change, 10 years +22.2%
Below its September 2022 peak -5.2%

The average house price in Greater Manchester, England, is £240,130 as of June 2026. This is what homes have cost there since January 1995, in today's money rather than in the cash prices of the day.

Over the last 10 years the average home in Greater Manchester has gained 22.2% in real terms, on a cash price 73.0% higher. Adjusted for inflation, prices in Greater Manchester peaked in September 2022 and are 5.2% below that today.
