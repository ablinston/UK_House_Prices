Where house prices are rising fastest in the UK, 2026

Every local authority in the UK, ranked from Land Registry data to June 2026. Real terms means in July 2026 money: the cash change less CPI inflation over the same five years. Areas are local authorities; the national and regional averages are not ranked.

Over the five years from June 2021, the average home rose most in real terms in Derry City and Strabane: +15.1% after inflation, on a cash rise of +47.3%. Of the 360 local authorities with a figure, 30 are up in real terms over those five years and 330 are down; the UK as a whole is -12.4%.
