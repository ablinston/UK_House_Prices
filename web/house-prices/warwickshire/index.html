Warwickshire house prices, adjusted for inflation

Average price, June 2026 £308,612

Below its February 2021 peak -9.8%

The average house price in Warwickshire, England, is £308,612 as of June 2026. This is what homes have cost there since January 1995, in today's money rather than in the cash prices of the day.

Over the last 10 years the average home in Warwickshire has lost 1.5% of its value in real terms, even though the cash price is 39.5% higher. Adjusted for inflation, prices in Warwickshire peaked in February 2021 and are 9.8% below that today.
