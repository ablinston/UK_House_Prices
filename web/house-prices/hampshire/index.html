Hampshire house prices, adjusted for inflation

Average price, June 2026 £364,273
Real change, 10 years -10.4%
Below its January 2022 peak -16.3%

The average house price in Hampshire, England, is £364,273 as of June 2026. This is what homes have cost there since January 1995, in today's money rather than in the cash prices of the day.

Over the last 10 years the average home in Hampshire has lost 10.4% of its value in real terms, even though the cash price is 26.9% higher. Adjusted for inflation, prices in Hampshire peaked in January 2022 and are 16.3% below that today.
