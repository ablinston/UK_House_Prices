East Midlands house prices, adjusted for inflation

Average price, June 2026 £240,462
Real change, 10 years +3.6%
Below its June 2021 peak -12.8%

The average house price in the East Midlands region of England is £240,462 as of June 2026. This is what homes have cost there since January 1995, in today's money rather than in the cash prices of the day.

Over the last 10 years the average home in East Midlands has gained 3.6% in real terms, on a cash price 46.8% higher. Adjusted for inflation, prices in East Midlands peaked in June 2021 and are 12.8% below that today.
