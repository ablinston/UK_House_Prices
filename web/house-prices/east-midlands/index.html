The average house price in the East Midlands region of England is £241,954 as of August 2026. This is what they have cost since January 1995, in today's money rather than in the cash prices of the day.

Over the last 10 years the average home in East Midlands has gained 2.3% in real terms, on a cash price 45.5% higher. Adjusted for inflation, home prices in East Midlands peaked in June 2021 and are 12.9% below that today.

The average detached price in the East Midlands region of England is £355,987 as of August 2026. This is what they have cost since January 1995, in today's money rather than in the cash prices of the day.

Over the last 10 years the average detached home in East Midlands has gained 0.4% in real terms, on a cash price 42.9% higher. Adjusted for inflation, detached home prices in East Midlands peaked in January 2022 and are 13.4% below that today.

The average semi-detached price in the East Midlands region of England is £231,096 as of August 2026. This is what they have cost since January 1995, in today's money rather than in the cash prices of the day.

Over the last 10 years the average semi-detached home in East Midlands has gained 5.2% in real terms, on a cash price 49.7% higher. Adjusted for inflation, semi-detached home prices in East Midlands peaked in September 2021 and are 11.2% below that today.

The average terraced price in the East Midlands region of England is £187,406 as of August 2026. This is what they have cost since January 1995, in today's money rather than in the cash prices of the day.

Over the last 10 years the average terraced home in East Midlands has gained 4.5% in real terms, on a cash price 48.7% higher. Adjusted for inflation, terraced home prices in East Midlands peaked in June 2021 and are 13.3% below that today.

The average flat price in the East Midlands region of England is £124,216 as of August 2026. This is what they have cost since January 1995, in today's money rather than in the cash prices of the day.

Over the last 10 years the average flat home in East Midlands has lost 9.7% of its value in real terms, even though the cash price is 28.5% higher. Adjusted for inflation, flat home prices in East Midlands peaked in September 2007 and are 27.8% below that today.
