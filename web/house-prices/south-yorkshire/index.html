The average house price in South Yorkshire, England, is £191,542 as of June 2026. This is what homes have cost there since January 1995, in today's money rather than in the cash prices of the day.

Over the last 10 years the average home in South Yorkshire has gained 9.2% in real terms, on a cash price 54.7% higher. Adjusted for inflation, home prices in South Yorkshire peaked in August 2007 and are 13.8% below that today.

The average detached price in South Yorkshire, England, is £299,055 as of June 2026. This is what homes have cost there since January 1995, in today's money rather than in the cash prices of the day.

Over the last 10 years the average detached home in South Yorkshire has gained 7.8% in real terms, on a cash price 52.7% higher. Adjusted for inflation, detached home prices in South Yorkshire peaked in August 2007 and are 13.0% below that today.

The average semi-detached price in South Yorkshire, England, is £193,508 as of June 2026. This is what homes have cost there since January 1995, in today's money rather than in the cash prices of the day.

Over the last 10 years the average semi-detached home in South Yorkshire has gained 11.6% in real terms, on a cash price 58.1% higher. Adjusted for inflation, semi-detached home prices in South Yorkshire peaked in August 2007 and are 10.8% below that today.

The average terraced price in South Yorkshire, England, is £160,458 as of June 2026. This is what homes have cost there since January 1995, in today's money rather than in the cash prices of the day.

Over the last 10 years the average terraced home in South Yorkshire has gained 11.1% in real terms, on a cash price 57.3% higher. Adjusted for inflation, terraced home prices in South Yorkshire peaked in August 2007 and are 12.7% below that today.

The average flat price in South Yorkshire, England, is £119,021 as of June 2026. This is what homes have cost there since January 1995, in today's money rather than in the cash prices of the day.

Over the last 10 years the average flat home in South Yorkshire has lost 3.8% of its value in real terms, even though the cash price is 36.2% higher. Adjusted for inflation, flat home prices in South Yorkshire peaked in July 2007 and are 30.1% below that today.
