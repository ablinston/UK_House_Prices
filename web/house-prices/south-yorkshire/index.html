The average house price in South Yorkshire, England, is £192,614 as of September 2026. This is what they have cost since January 1995, in today's money rather than in the cash prices of the day.

Over the last 10 years the average home in South Yorkshire has gained 7.8% in real terms, on a cash price 53.6% higher. Adjusted for inflation, home prices in South Yorkshire peaked in August 2007 and are 14.3% below that today.

The average semi-detached price in South Yorkshire, England, is £194,738 as of September 2026. This is what they have cost since January 1995, in today's money rather than in the cash prices of the day.

The average terraced price in South Yorkshire, England, is £161,471 as of September 2026. This is what they have cost since January 1995, in today's money rather than in the cash prices of the day.

The average flat price in South Yorkshire, England, is £118,655 as of September 2026. This is what they have cost since January 1995, in today's money rather than in the cash prices of the day.

Over the last 10 years the average flat home in South Yorkshire has lost 6.6% of its value in real terms, even though the cash price is 33.2% higher. Adjusted for inflation, flat home prices in South Yorkshire peaked in July 2007 and are 31.1% below that today.
