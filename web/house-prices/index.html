UK house prices by area, adjusted for inflation

UK average price, June 2026 £272,187
Real change, 10 years -2.0%
Below its September 2021 peak -12.5%

The average house price across the UK is £272,187 as of June 2026. This is what homes have cost since January 1995, in today's money rather than in the cash prices of the day.

Over the last 10 years the average home in United Kingdom has lost 2.0% of its value in real terms, even though the cash price is 38.8% higher. Adjusted for inflation, prices in United Kingdom peaked in September 2021 and are 12.5% below that today.
