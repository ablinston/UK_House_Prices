The average house price across the UK is £272,187 as of June 2026. This is what they have cost since January 1995, in today's money rather than in the cash prices of the day.

Over the last 10 years the average home in United Kingdom has lost 2.0% of its value in real terms, even though the cash price is 38.8% higher. Adjusted for inflation, home prices in United Kingdom peaked in September 2021 and are 12.5% below that today.

The average detached price across the UK is £444,608 as of June 2026. This is what they have cost since January 2005, in today's money rather than in the cash prices of the day.

Over the last 10 years the average detached home in United Kingdom has lost 0.8% of its value in real terms, even though the cash price is 40.6% higher. Adjusted for inflation, detached home prices in United Kingdom peaked in January 2022 and are 12.0% below that today.

The average semi-detached price across the UK is £277,303 as of June 2026. This is what they have cost since January 2005, in today's money rather than in the cash prices of the day.

Over the last 10 years the average semi-detached home in United Kingdom has gained 3.3% in real terms, on a cash price 46.3% higher. Adjusted for inflation, semi-detached home prices in United Kingdom peaked in September 2021 and are 9.7% below that today.

The average terraced price across the UK is £230,968 as of June 2026. This is what they have cost since January 2005, in today's money rather than in the cash prices of the day.

Over the last 10 years the average terraced home in United Kingdom has gained 1.8% in real terms, on a cash price 44.3% higher. Adjusted for inflation, terraced home prices in United Kingdom peaked in June 2021 and are 11.6% below that today.

The average flat price across the UK is £193,689 as of June 2026. This is what they have cost since January 2005, in today's money rather than in the cash prices of the day.

Over the last 10 years the average flat home in United Kingdom has lost 15.8% of its value in real terms, even though the cash price is 19.3% higher. Adjusted for inflation, flat home prices in United Kingdom peaked in June 2021 and are 20.7% below that today.
