The average house price across the UK is £272,647 as of September 2026. This is what they have cost since January 1995, in today's money rather than in the cash prices of the day.

Over the last 10 years the average home in United Kingdom has lost 3.3% of its value in real terms, even though the cash price is 37.8% higher. Adjusted for inflation, home prices in United Kingdom peaked in September 2021 and are 13.3% below that today.

The average detached price across the UK is £447,647 as of September 2026. This is what they have cost since January 2005, in today's money rather than in the cash prices of the day.

Over the last 10 years the average detached home in United Kingdom has lost 1.9% of its value in real terms, even though the cash price is 39.9% higher. Adjusted for inflation, detached home prices in United Kingdom peaked in January 2022 and are 12.4% below that today.

The average semi-detached price across the UK is £278,414 as of September 2026. This is what they have cost since January 2005, in today's money rather than in the cash prices of the day.

Over the last 10 years the average semi-detached home in United Kingdom has gained 1.9% in real terms, on a cash price 45.2% higher. Adjusted for inflation, semi-detached home prices in United Kingdom peaked in September 2021 and are 10.4% below that today.

The average terraced price across the UK is £231,521 as of September 2026. This is what they have cost since January 2005, in today's money rather than in the cash prices of the day.

Over the last 10 years the average terraced home in United Kingdom has gained 1.0% in real terms, on a cash price 44.0% higher. Adjusted for inflation, terraced home prices in United Kingdom peaked in June 2021 and are 12.3% below that today.

The average flat price across the UK is £191,867 as of September 2026. This is what they have cost since January 2005, in today's money rather than in the cash prices of the day.

Over the last 10 years the average flat home in United Kingdom has lost 17.9% of its value in real terms, even though the cash price is 17.0% higher. Adjusted for inflation, flat home prices in United Kingdom peaked in June 2021 and are 22.3% below that today.
