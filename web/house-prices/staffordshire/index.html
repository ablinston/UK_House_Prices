The average house price in Staffordshire, England, is £250,987 as of September 2026. This is what they have cost since January 1995, in today's money rather than in the cash prices of the day.

Over the last 10 years the average home in Staffordshire has gained 4.2% in real terms, on a cash price 48.5% higher. Adjusted for inflation, home prices in Staffordshire peaked in August 2007 and are 10.3% below that today.

The average flat price in Staffordshire, England, is £121,243 as of September 2026. This is what they have cost since January 1995, in today's money rather than in the cash prices of the day.
