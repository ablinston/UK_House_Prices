The average house price in Staffordshire, England, is £251,130 as of August 2026. This is what they have cost since January 1995, in today's money rather than in the cash prices of the day.

Over the last 10 years the average home in Staffordshire has gained 4.8% in real terms, on a cash price 49.1% higher. Adjusted for inflation, home prices in Staffordshire peaked in August 2007 and are 9.9% below that today.

The average detached price in Staffordshire, England, is £389,122 as of August 2026. This is what they have cost since January 1995, in today's money rather than in the cash prices of the day.

The average semi-detached price in Staffordshire, England, is £240,916 as of August 2026. This is what they have cost since January 1995, in today's money rather than in the cash prices of the day.

The average terraced price in Staffordshire, England, is £190,077 as of August 2026. This is what they have cost since January 1995, in today's money rather than in the cash prices of the day.

Over the last 10 years the average terraced home in Staffordshire has gained 6.0% in real terms, on a cash price 50.8% higher. Adjusted for inflation, terraced home prices in Staffordshire peaked in August 2007 and are 10.2% below that today.

The average flat price in Staffordshire, England, is £121,511 as of August 2026. This is what they have cost since January 1995, in today's money rather than in the cash prices of the day.

Over the last 10 years the average flat home in Staffordshire has lost 8.7% of its value in real terms, even though the cash price is 29.9% higher. Adjusted for inflation, flat home prices in Staffordshire peaked in July 2007 and are 30.1% below that today.
