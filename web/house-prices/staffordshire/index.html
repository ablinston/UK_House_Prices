The average house price in Staffordshire, England, is £250,653 as of July 2026. This is what they have cost since January 1995, in today's money rather than in the cash prices of the day.

The average flat price in Staffordshire, England, is £120,885 as of July 2026. This is what they have cost since January 1995, in today's money rather than in the cash prices of the day.
