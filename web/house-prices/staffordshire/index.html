The average house price in Staffordshire, England, is £250,606 as of June 2026. This is what homes have cost there since January 1995, in today's money rather than in the cash prices of the day.

Over the last 10 years the average home in Staffordshire has gained 6.8% in real terms, on a cash price 51.3% higher. Adjusted for inflation, home prices in Staffordshire peaked in August 2007 and are 9.4% below that today.

The average detached price in Staffordshire, England, is £389,277 as of June 2026. This is what homes have cost there since January 1995, in today's money rather than in the cash prices of the day.

Over the last 10 years the average detached home in Staffordshire has gained 6.0% in real terms, on a cash price 50.2% higher. Adjusted for inflation, detached home prices in Staffordshire peaked in January 2008 and are 7.8% below that today.

The average semi-detached price in Staffordshire, England, is £240,435 as of June 2026. This is what homes have cost there since January 1995, in today's money rather than in the cash prices of the day.

Over the last 10 years the average semi-detached home in Staffordshire has gained 9.9% in real terms, on a cash price 55.7% higher. Adjusted for inflation, semi-detached home prices in Staffordshire peaked in August 2007 and are 6.1% below that today.

The average terraced price in Staffordshire, England, is £188,778 as of June 2026. This is what homes have cost there since January 1995, in today's money rather than in the cash prices of the day.

Over the last 10 years the average terraced home in Staffordshire has gained 7.4% in real terms, on a cash price 52.1% higher. Adjusted for inflation, terraced home prices in Staffordshire peaked in August 2007 and are 10.1% below that today.

The average flat price in Staffordshire, England, is £121,123 as of June 2026. This is what homes have cost there since January 1995, in today's money rather than in the cash prices of the day.

Over the last 10 years the average flat home in Staffordshire has lost 7.3% of its value in real terms, even though the cash price is 31.3% higher. Adjusted for inflation, flat home prices in Staffordshire peaked in July 2007 and are 29.8% below that today.
