The average house price in Staffordshire, England, is £250,606 as of June 2026. This is what they have cost since January 1995, in today's money rather than in the cash prices of the day.

Over the last 10 years the average home in Staffordshire has gained 6.8% in real terms, on a cash price 51.3% higher. Adjusted for inflation, home prices in Staffordshire peaked in August 2007 and are 9.4% below that today.

The average flat price in Staffordshire, England, is £121,123 as of June 2026. This is what they have cost since January 1995, in today's money rather than in the cash prices of the day.
