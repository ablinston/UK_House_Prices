The average house price in the East of England region of England is £338,720 as of June 2026. This is what they have cost since January 1995, in today's money rather than in the cash prices of the day.

Over the last 10 years the average home in East of England has lost 9.6% of its value in real terms, even though the cash price is 28.1% higher. Adjusted for inflation, home prices in East of England peaked in September 2021 and are 15.7% below that today.
