The average house price in the East of England region of England is £337,542 as of July 2026. This is what they have cost since January 1995, in today's money rather than in the cash prices of the day.

Over the last 10 years the average home in East of England has lost 11.3% of its value in real terms, even though the cash price is 26.0% higher. Adjusted for inflation, home prices in East of England peaked in September 2021 and are 16.2% below that today.
