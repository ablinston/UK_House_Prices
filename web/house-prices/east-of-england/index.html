East of England house prices, adjusted for inflation

The average house price in the East of England region of England is £338,720 as of June 2026. This is what homes have cost there since January 1995, in today's money rather than in the cash prices of the day.

Average price, June 2026 £338,720
Real change since the peak -5.9%

The average home in East of England costs 5.9% less in real terms than at the September 2007 peak, even though the cash price is 63.7% higher.
