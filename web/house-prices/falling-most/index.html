Where house prices have fallen most in the UK, 2026

Every local authority in the UK, ranked from Land Registry data to July 2026. Real terms means in August 2026 money: the cash change less CPI inflation over the same five years. Areas are local authorities; the national and regional averages are not ranked.

Over the five years from July 2021, the average home fell most in real terms in City of London: -36.9% after inflation, on a cash fall of -19.0%. Of the 360 local authorities with a figure, 19 are up in real terms over those five years and 341 are down; the UK as a whole is -8.2%.
