Where house prices have fallen most in the UK, 2026

Every local authority in the UK, ranked from Land Registry data to September 2026. Real terms means in October 2026 money: the cash change less CPI inflation over the same five years. Areas are local authorities; the national and regional averages are not ranked.

Over the five years from September 2021, the average home fell most in real terms in Kensington and Chelsea: -44.3% after inflation, on a cash fall of -28.6%. Of the 360 local authorities with a figure, 18 are up in real terms over those five years and 342 are down; the UK as a whole is -13.3%.
