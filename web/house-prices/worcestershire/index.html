The average house price in Worcestershire, England, is £289,400 as of June 2026. This is what they have cost there since January 1995, in today's money rather than in the cash prices of the day.

Over the last 10 years the average home in Worcestershire has gained 0.9% in real terms, on a cash price 42.9% higher. Adjusted for inflation, home prices in Worcestershire peaked in October 2007 and are 13.2% below that today.

The average terraced price in Worcestershire, England, is £226,695 as of June 2026. This is what they have cost there since January 1995, in today's money rather than in the cash prices of the day.

The average flat price in Worcestershire, England, is £142,303 as of June 2026. This is what they have cost there since January 1995, in today's money rather than in the cash prices of the day.
