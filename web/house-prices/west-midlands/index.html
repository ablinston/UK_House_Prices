The average house price in West Midlands, England, is £236,043 as of August 2026. This is what they have cost since January 1995, in today's money rather than in the cash prices of the day.

Over the last 10 years the average home in West Midlands has gained 6.4% in real terms, on a cash price 51.4% higher. Adjusted for inflation, home prices in West Midlands peaked in September 2022 and are 9.8% below that today.

The average semi-detached price in West Midlands, England, is £260,294 as of August 2026. This is what they have cost since January 1995, in today's money rather than in the cash prices of the day.

The average terraced price in West Midlands, England, is £212,623 as of August 2026. This is what they have cost since January 1995, in today's money rather than in the cash prices of the day.

The average flat price in West Midlands, England, is £138,695 as of August 2026. This is what they have cost since January 1995, in today's money rather than in the cash prices of the day.

Over the last 10 years the average flat home in West Midlands has lost 8.2% of its value in real terms, even though the cash price is 30.7% higher. Adjusted for inflation, flat home prices in West Midlands peaked in July 2007 and are 25.5% below that today.
