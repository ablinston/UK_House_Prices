The average house price in West Midlands, England, is £234,750 as of June 2026. This is what they have cost since January 1995, in today's money rather than in the cash prices of the day.

Over the last 10 years the average home in West Midlands has gained 8.1% in real terms, on a cash price 53.1% higher. Adjusted for inflation, home prices in West Midlands peaked in September 2022 and are 9.6% below that today.

The average flat price in West Midlands, England, is £139,757 as of June 2026. This is what they have cost since January 1995, in today's money rather than in the cash prices of the day.
