The average house price in West Midlands, England, is £237,164 as of September 2026. This is what they have cost since January 1995, in today's money rather than in the cash prices of the day.

Over the last 10 years the average home in West Midlands has gained 6.4% in real terms, on a cash price 51.6% higher. Adjusted for inflation, home prices in West Midlands peaked in September 2022 and are 9.7% below that today.

The average flat price in West Midlands, England, is £139,274 as of September 2026. This is what they have cost since January 1995, in today's money rather than in the cash prices of the day.
