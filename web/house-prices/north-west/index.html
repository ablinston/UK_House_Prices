The average house price in the North West region of England is £220,489 as of September 2026. This is what they have cost since January 1995, in today's money rather than in the cash prices of the day.

Over the last 10 years the average home in North West has gained 10.8% in real terms, on a cash price 57.9% higher. Adjusted for inflation, home prices in North West peaked in August 2007 and are 11.2% below that today.

The average detached price in the North West region of England is £391,993 as of September 2026. This is what they have cost since January 1995, in today's money rather than in the cash prices of the day.

Over the last 10 years the average detached home in North West has gained 7.4% in real terms, on a cash price 53.1% higher. Adjusted for inflation, detached home prices in North West peaked in October 2007 and are 10.4% below that today.

The average semi-detached price in the North West region of England is £242,310 as of September 2026. This is what they have cost since January 1995, in today's money rather than in the cash prices of the day.

The average terraced price in the North West region of England is £177,883 as of September 2026. This is what they have cost since January 1995, in today's money rather than in the cash prices of the day.

Over the last 10 years the average terraced home in North West has gained 15.2% in real terms, on a cash price 64.2% higher. Adjusted for inflation, terraced home prices in North West peaked in August 2007 and are 10.1% below that today.

The average flat price in the North West region of England is £138,965 as of September 2026. This is what they have cost since January 1995, in today's money rather than in the cash prices of the day.

Over the last 10 years the average flat home in North West has lost 1.6% of its value in real terms, even though the cash price is 40.2% higher. Adjusted for inflation, flat home prices in North West peaked in July 2007 and are 26.0% below that today.
