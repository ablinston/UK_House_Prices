Average price, June 2026 £315,760

Below its September 2022 peak -21.4%

The average house price in East Sussex, England, is £315,760 as of June 2026. This is what homes have cost there since January 1995, in today's money rather than in the cash prices of the day.

Over the last 10 years the average home in East Sussex has lost 9.9% of its value in real terms, even though the cash price is 27.7% higher. Adjusted for inflation, prices in East Sussex peaked in September 2022 and are 21.4% below that today.
