The average house price in Wales is £214,568 as of September 2026. This is what they have cost since January 1995, in today's money rather than in the cash prices of the day.

Over the last 10 years the average home in Wales has gained 9.7% in real terms, on a cash price 56.3% higher. Adjusted for inflation, home prices in Wales peaked in August 2007 and are 14.4% below that today.

The average detached price in Wales is £337,609 as of September 2026. This is what they have cost since January 1995, in today's money rather than in the cash prices of the day.

Over the last 10 years the average detached home in Wales has gained 8.0% in real terms, on a cash price 53.9% higher. Adjusted for inflation, detached home prices in Wales peaked in August 2007 and are 14.1% below that today.

The average semi-detached price in Wales is £213,616 as of September 2026. This is what they have cost since January 1995, in today's money rather than in the cash prices of the day.

Over the last 10 years the average semi-detached home in Wales has gained 11.7% in real terms, on a cash price 59.2% higher. Adjusted for inflation, semi-detached home prices in Wales peaked in August 2007 and are 11.0% below that today.

The average terraced price in Wales is £171,892 as of September 2026. This is what they have cost since January 1995, in today's money rather than in the cash prices of the day.

Over the last 10 years the average terraced home in Wales has gained 13.3% in real terms, on a cash price 61.5% higher. Adjusted for inflation, terraced home prices in Wales peaked in August 2007 and are 13.1% below that today.

The average flat price in Wales is £127,453 as of September 2026. This is what they have cost since January 1995, in today's money rather than in the cash prices of the day.

Over the last 10 years the average flat home in Wales has lost 6.6% of its value in real terms, even though the cash price is 33.1% higher. Adjusted for inflation, flat home prices in Wales peaked in September 2007 and are 32.0% below that today.
