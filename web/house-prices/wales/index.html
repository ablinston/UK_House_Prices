The average house price in Wales is £215,017 as of July 2026. This is what they have cost since January 1995, in today's money rather than in the cash prices of the day.

Over the last 10 years the average home in Wales has gained 9.2% in real terms, on a cash price 55.1% higher. Adjusted for inflation, home prices in Wales peaked in August 2007 and are 13.5% below that today.

The average detached price in Wales is £336,272 as of July 2026. This is what they have cost since January 1995, in today's money rather than in the cash prices of the day.

Over the last 10 years the average detached home in Wales has gained 7.8% in real terms, on a cash price 53.1% higher. Adjusted for inflation, detached home prices in Wales peaked in August 2007 and are 13.7% below that today.

The average semi-detached price in Wales is £215,078 as of July 2026. This is what they have cost since January 1995, in today's money rather than in the cash prices of the day.

Over the last 10 years the average semi-detached home in Wales has gained 12.0% in real terms, on a cash price 59.2% higher. Adjusted for inflation, semi-detached home prices in Wales peaked in August 2007 and are 9.7% below that today.

The average terraced price in Wales is £172,262 as of July 2026. This is what they have cost since January 1995, in today's money rather than in the cash prices of the day.

Over the last 10 years the average terraced home in Wales has gained 11.8% in real terms, on a cash price 58.9% higher. Adjusted for inflation, terraced home prices in Wales peaked in August 2007 and are 12.2% below that today.

The average flat price in Wales is £128,581 as of July 2026. This is what they have cost since January 1995, in today's money rather than in the cash prices of the day.

Over the last 10 years the average flat home in Wales has lost 6.5% of its value in real terms, even though the cash price is 32.8% higher. Adjusted for inflation, flat home prices in Wales peaked in September 2007 and are 30.9% below that today.
