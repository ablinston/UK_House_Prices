South West house prices, adjusted for inflation

The average house price in the South West region of England is £304,545 as of June 2026. This is what homes have cost there since January 1995, in today's money rather than in the cash prices of the day.

Average price, June 2026 £304,545
Real change since the peak -14.7%

The average home in South West costs 14.7% less in real terms than at the September 2007 peak, even though the cash price is 48.4% higher.
