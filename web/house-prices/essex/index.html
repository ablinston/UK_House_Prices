Essex house prices, adjusted for inflation

Average price, June 2026 £361,278
Real change, 10 years -8.7%
Below its January 2022 peak -15.1%

The average house price in Essex, England, is £361,278 as of June 2026. This is what homes have cost there since January 1995, in today's money rather than in the cash prices of the day.

Over the last 10 years the average home in Essex has lost 8.7% of its value in real terms, even though the cash price is 29.4% higher. Adjusted for inflation, prices in Essex peaked in January 2022 and are 15.1% below that today.
