Average price, June 2026 £470,852

Below its November 2016 peak -16.8%

The average house price in Hertfordshire, England, is £470,852 as of June 2026. This is what homes have cost there since January 1995, in today's money rather than in the cash prices of the day.

Over the last 10 years the average home in Hertfordshire has lost 15.1% of its value in real terms, even though the cash price is 20.2% higher. Adjusted for inflation, prices in Hertfordshire peaked in November 2016 and are 16.8% below that today.
